Workspace Storage & Portability

Dayline stores your data on this device in this browser. After the first online visit, the app shell can open offline. Export a JSON snapshot to back up or transfer your workspace; offline edits do not sync between devices.

Allow notifications for reminders and focus sessions. Scheduled reminders are checked while Dayline is open; reliable background delivery requires platform push support and cannot be guaranteed by a static offline app.
